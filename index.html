01 About

Rooted in Khulna,
drawn to everywhere else.

I’m a student from Rangpur City, living in Khulna, and I have never quite managed to stay still for long.

The profile I keep public says a few plain things: I live in Khulna, I’m from Rangpur City, I study at Khulna University, and travelling is the hobby I return to most. It also lists music as an interest, chess and cricket among the teams I follow, and a handful of pages I keep close. That is an honest snapshot — not a résumé.

So this page is not a list of achievements. It is a small, well-kept room. A place for the photographs from the road, the songs that carried a long journey home, and the ordinary afternoons that would otherwise disappear. Some corners are filled in; the ones that are not are clearly marked, waiting for the right photograph or the right story.
